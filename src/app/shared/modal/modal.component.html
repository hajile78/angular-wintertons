Lord, as the years go by, grant me restraint of tongue and a patient heart; help me speak when my words heal and be silent when they harm.

Let me be helpful without overbearing, thoughtful without gloom, and humble in the sharing of whatever hard-won wisdom I have.

Keep me gentle in temper, quick to encourage, slow to complain, and grateful for small mercies each day.

Amen
